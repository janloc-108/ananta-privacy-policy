1. Introduction

ANANTA CREATIONS ("we", "our", or "us") provides the Ganesha Oracle mobile application. We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how the application handles information.

Ganesha Oracle does not require account registration or the submission of personal information to use its core features.

The application is designed to provide illustrated Ganesha wisdom cards, inspirational messages, and related content without requesting your name, email address, telephone number, or location.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

Certain preferences, including saved favorite messages and application settings, may be stored locally on your device to support the application's functionality.

This locally stored information is not intentionally transmitted to ANANTA CREATIONS.

4. Advertising and Analytics

Ganesha Oracle does not contain third-party advertising.

The current version is not designed to use third-party advertising or behavioral tracking services.

5. Permissions

The application does not require access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location for its core functionality.

6. Third-Party Services

When the application is installed or updated through Google Play, Google may process certain device and technical information according to its own privacy policies.

For more information, please visit Google's Privacy Policy .

7. Children's Privacy

Ganesha Oracle does not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children. The application is intended for general inspirational and reflective use.

8. Data Security

We aim to minimize the information required to operate the application. Preferences stored locally on your device remain subject to your device's security and backup settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features or applicable requirements change.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact ANANTA CREATIONS through the developer contact information provided on the application's Google Play listing.
